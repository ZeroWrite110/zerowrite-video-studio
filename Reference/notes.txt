Studied for style only. Nothing is copied.

Videos to look at (YouTube):
- CrowdStrike "Welcome to CrowdStrike" (endpoint protection): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIWxJzPfpyY
  Calm, direct voice. Starts with the outcome ("We stop breaches"). Motion graphics and platform diagrams. Credibility before cleverness.
- SentinelOne "Singularity Platform": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B-Uv8IJAo_Y
  Kinetic typography, one consistent graphic system.
- NinjaOne endpoint security (1 min, flat vector): https://youtu.be/v8eMEdRC3MY
- Huntress (1 min 30 s, comic-style illustration, plain-English tone): https://youtu.be/xFpZxupyb4c
- ThreatLocker "Introducing ThreatLocker in 60 seconds" and "Allowlisting in 4 minutes" (closest competitor idea: block what is not approved): https://www.threatlocker.com/resources/videos

Patterns that work (from Vidico and ContentBeta 2026 roundups):
- Lead with the consequence, not the technology.
- Arc: problem -> consequence -> solution -> proof -> one clear next step.
- 60 s to 2 min for website explainers; 20-30 s cuts for paid social / Reels.
- Product shown as clean UI screens after the problem is set up.
- Fast typography and icons for lists; one colour system throughout.

How ZeroWrite stands apart visually:
- Competitors use red/black "threat" palettes. ZeroWrite's site is dark ink + cyan, calm and technical.
- Our strongest visual is the website's own "intent score" panel (+14, +32, +28 ... = 94, quarantined). Animate it.
